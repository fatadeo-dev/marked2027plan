How to use this page: read each section and tap Discuss to comment. Where we must decide something (date, 1 or 2 days, breakout format), go to Have Your Say and vote with your reason.

1. Fix the date first

Avoid WAEC/NECO (roughly May–June) and the JAMB UTME window (usually April–May, so check the 2027 timetable). Avoid term exams and the Christmas period.

3. The committee: six workstreams

Choose where you will serve on the Team tab. You are free to form your own workstream team: pick people you work well with, or ask the Director for suggestions. Most roles need 1–2 hours a week, more in the final month.

5. Parents & Local ChurchesSee the note belowPastoral/family ministry1–2 hrs/wk
6. Media, Online Groups & FinancePublicity, photos (with consent), WhatsApp channel; budget, sponsorship, accountsMedia/design, accounting, fundraising1–2 hrs/wk
What "Local Churches" means: this workstream works through the local churches in two ways. (1) Before the program, it gets pastors to announce the program and invite teens and parents. (2) It helps each church name a teen mentor pair who will follow up teens after the day. The parents' session itself happens at the event. Parent seminars in local churches come later, as a follow-up series after MARKED 2027.

4. Deliverables for Meeting 1

Each item is a short draft (a one-page note or a list), not a finished product. Track and update them on the Tracker.

  • Director: confirmed date (or top two options); budget ceiling; who is on which workstream
  • Program: 2 possible speakers per main session; draft list of breakout facilitators
  • Counselling & Safeguarding: 6–8 possible counsellors; draft consent form; "if a teen discloses harm" card
  • Logistics: 2–3 venue options compared; feeding cost per head; transport plan per district
  • Registration: one coordinator per district; draft registration form
  • Parents & Local Churches: draft message to pastors; 2 speakers for the parents' session
  • Media & Finance: budget template; 3 sponsorship ideas; save-the-date text

7. Meeting 1 agenda (90 minutes)

  1. Opening prayer & 5 key findings from the teens (10 min)
  2. Confirm the date; walk through the program and the vote results (15 min)
  3. Workstream report-backs, 5 min each (35 min)
  4. Decisions: venue, budget ceiling, first speakers to invite (15 min)
  5. Next deliverables & date of Meeting 2 (10 min)
  6. Closing prayer (5 min)
